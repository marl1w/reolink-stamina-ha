## What that means in practice

- **Issues and pull requests** are read by one person in their own time. A clear reproduction and the output of [debug logging](docs/faq.md#turning-on-debug-logging) is the difference between a fix and a conversation.
- **Hardware reports are especially useful.** This is developed against an RLN8-410 and the cameras listed in the README. Other *models* of recorder are where the surprises live, so "it works on mine" is a genuinely valuable issue to open, as is the opposite.
- **Diagnostics beat screenshots.** *Settings → Devices & services → Reolink Stamina → ⋮ → Download diagnostics* collects what is normally needed and names no camera.

## Security

Something that should not be discussed in a public issue — a way to read footage without permission, or credentials leaking somewhere they should not — is best raised through GitHub's private vulnerability reporting on the repository rather than in the issue tracker.
